# S3-Benchmarks Frontier Lab V1

S3-Benchmarks is an independent evidence lab.

Its role is not to prove S3 is fast. Its role is to discover:

- what S3 can execute;
- what it cannot execute;
- where performance pressure exists;
- why it exists;
- whether it is causal;
- which experiment should happen next.

The lab preserves provenance, correctness contracts, reproducible protocols,
and explicit uncertainty. Diagnostic artifacts are never production claims.
